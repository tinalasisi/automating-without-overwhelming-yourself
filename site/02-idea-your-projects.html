The IDEA framework

2 min

IDEA is not a building plan. It is a thinking tool.

IIdentify

  • What are you trying to accomplish?
  • The goal is not automation. The goal is the desired outcome.
  • What does "done" look like?

DDocument

  • Break the task into building blocks: data source, transformation, output.
  • What's fixed? What varies?

EExperiment

  • Choose one component to test.
  • Start with the smallest workable version.
  • See how it performs in your real context.

AAdjust

  • What could break if something changes?
  • Who else is affected?
  • What ongoing maintenance is required?

Example: the Monday attendance email

  1. Pull data from a sign-in sheet
  2. Copy into an email and format it
  3. Send it every Monday
  4. About 20 minutes a week
Run the example through IDEA
Identify
The goal is not "automate the attendance email." That is a solution. The goal: the team gets an accurate, timely summary every week without me assembling it. Trigger: the start of a new week. Done means a formatted email in inboxes by Monday at 10 AM.
Document
Source: the sign-in sheet. Transformation: pull the numbers, maybe calculate percentages. Output: the email. Fixed: format, recipients, structure. Varies: the numbers, the date, who was absent.
Experiment
Auto-fill a template with this week's numbers and still send it by hand. Test one piece and see if it works.
Adjust
What if someone changes the sign-in sheet? What if a new person joins the team? How would I know something went wrong?

Some tasks are fine the way they are. That is a valid outcome.
